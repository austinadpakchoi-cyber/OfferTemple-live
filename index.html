01

Product positioning & goals

Positioning: Deliver an English-first Huangli experience: daily DO/DON'T, auspicious hours, and one-line cultural context, supported by subscriptions and lightweight decision tools.

Business goals (first 3 months):

  • Grow email/Telegram subscriber list to 5,000+.
  • Reach 300 monthly sales for the $19 Digital Pack.
  • Achieve 3% conversion from the 7-day free trial to paid.
  • Maintain ≥ 50% organic traffic from SEO and social channels.

Primary audience: Global users curious about East Asian culture, metaphysics, or productivity rituals. Motivations include actionable daily reminders, culturally grounded insights, and seasonal storytelling.

03

MVP feature set

3.1 Daily Huangli card

  • Fields: date, dayOfficer, do[], dont[], clashAnimal, hours[], why.
  • Display: DO/DON'T list, auspicious hours, one-line context, CTA to free trial and Digital Pack.
  • Subscriptions: email, Telegram (SMS later), ICS feed via /api/ics.

3.2 Chinese Zodiac Finder

  • Input: birth date (optional time & timezone), returns zodiac name, icon, quick traits, clash guidance.
  • Logic handles lunar new year boundary using local timezone or Beijing-time override.
  • Mapping: (year − 4) % 12 → Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig.
  • Edge-case toggle: “Use Beijing time for boundary birthdays.”

3.3 Lunar Date Converter

  • Input solar date → return lunar year/month/day, zodiac, solar term (if any), with copy-to-clipboard.
  • MVP can rely on pre-computed table or trusted library; long-term server-side calculation with caching.

3.4 Free Trial (7 days)

  • Email capture with consent checkbox.
  • Automation: welcome email, daily sends for 6 days, day-7 conversion push.

3.5 Checkout & fulfillment

  • Payments: Stripe first, PayPal optional later.
  • Delivery: instant PDF download + ICS link, add buyer to 60-day nurture sequence.

3.6 ICS subscription

  • All-day events with DO/DON'T summary in the title and metadata in description.
  • Monthly rolling updates, primarily supporting email subscribers.

06

Backend API sketch

  • GET /api/daily?date=YYYY-MM-DD → Daily card JSON.
  • GET /api/ics?days=30 → ICS feed.
  • POST /api/subscribe { email } → Store signup & trigger 7-day automation.
  • POST /api/checkout → Stripe Checkout Session.
  • POST /api/webhooks/stripe → Fulfillment and nurture sequence enrollment.
  • GET /api/zodiac?date=YYYY-MM-DD&tz=America/Los_Angeles&mode=beijing|local
  • GET /api/lunar?date=YYYY-MM-DD&tz=...
07

Tech stack & infrastructure

  • Frontend/SSR: Next.js (App Router) + React.
  • Styling: Minimal CSS for MVP → Tailwind + design system later.
  • Data: JSON files initially, upgrade to Postgres/Supabase or CMS.
  • Email: Manual/CSV for MVP → ESP integration (Resend, SES, MailerLite, etc.).
  • Payments: Stripe first, optional PayPal.
  • Hosting: Node + PM2 + Nginx or Vercel/Netlify.
  • Monitoring: Plausible/GA4, server logs via PM2.
08

Content & tone guidelines

  • DO/DON'T: Start with verbs, maximum six words, avoid strong medical/financial promises.
  • Why copy: 25 words or fewer, emphasize cultural rationale.
  • Voice: Gentle, actionable, culture-forward.
  • Universal disclaimer: For cultural & entertainment purposes only. Not medical, financial or legal advice.
09

SEO & growth strategy

  • Implement structured data: FAQPage for FAQ content, Article schema for glossary entries.
  • Target long-tail keywords: solar terms, Day Officers, Zodiac guides, tool pages.
  • Create shareable OG images for daily cards and tool outputs.
  • Funnel: tools → free trial email capture → daily drip → purchase.
10

Compliance & risk

  • No medical, investment, or legal guarantees; avoid “guaranteed” language.
  • Email compliance: include unsubscribe links, transparent privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp/SMS only with explicit opt-in and clear STOP instructions.
  • Be mindful of regional restrictions for fortune-telling content; rely on organic distribution.

MVP acceptance criteria

  • Zodiac Finder & Lunar Converter deliver accurate results, including lunar new year boundaries.
